Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Dual-Engine SEO, Generative Engine Optimization, and our editorial workflow.

What is the difference between traditional SEO and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?
Traditional SEO focuses on ranking links on Google's search results page. GEO optimizes content structure, entity relationships, statistical citations, and JSON-LD schema so large language models (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI Overviews) directly quote and recommend your brand in synthesized answers.
How does JetDigitalPro ensure content passes Google Spam policies?
We never publish raw AI text. Every article undergoes deep SERP gap research, original data enrichment, and line-by-line rewriting by niche-specialized human editors to satisfy Google's E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) guidelines.
